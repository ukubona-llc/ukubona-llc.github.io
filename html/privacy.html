Privacy Policy

Ukubona LLC is committed to protecting your privacy and honoring your data rights. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard information through our public-facing tools and platforms.

1. What We Collect

Ukubona does not collect personally identifiable information unless you explicitly provide it (e.g. via email).

  • Our risk calculators run entirely in-browser. No input data is transmitted or stored on our servers.
  • We do not use cookies for advertising or profiling purposes.
  • Basic anonymized analytics (e.g. page visits) may be collected via GitHub Pages infrastructure or Google Analytics.

2. Use of Cookies

Cookies may be used to remember your light/dark mode preference or to keep layout settings consistent across visits. You can disable cookies in your browser at any time without affecting core functionality.

3. Third-Party Links

Some pages may link to external resources, such as academic articles, GitHub repositories, or collaborator websites. These sites are governed by their own privacy policies, and we recommend reviewing them before interacting.

4. California and CCPA Compliance

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including:

  • The right to know what personal information is collected
  • The right to request deletion of your information
  • The right to opt out of data sharing

To exercise your rights or ask questions about your data, please email us directly at ukubona.llc@gmail.com.

5. Future Updates

This policy may evolve as Ukubona’s platform and products grow. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised date stamp.
