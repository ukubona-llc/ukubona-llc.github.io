Our Vision

Founded in March 2025, Ukubona LLC transforms statistical models into interactive, personalized visualizations that empower informed decision-making under uncertainty. We specialize in high-stakes domains like living kidney donation, perioperative risk, and metabolic self-regulation, building tools where consent becomes process, information becomes knowledge, and personalized medicine begins with perspective.
