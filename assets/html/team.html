Meet the Team

Technologists, academics, and clinicians weaving insight into action

Meet Us

Our Ethos

At Ukubona, to see is to engage in a dynamic process — sensing patterns, weaving memory, applying meaning, and taking action. Our team spans continents and disciplines, united by a shared commitment to building decision systems that reflect the recursive, embodied nature of human perception.

Leadership

Abimereki Muzaale, MD, MPH
Founder and CEO

Dr. Muzaale has been an NIH-funded clinical investigator for over 15 years with grants from NIDDK and NIA. He oversees initiatives focusing on decision-support and personalized risk-assessment, blending clinical insight with data-driven innovation.

Core Staff

Vincent Jin, MS
Analyst – Data Visualization & Strategy

Vincent builds the future of personalized medicine. Drawing on a deep knowledge of transplant databases, Cox models, and uncertainty quantification, he designs platforms delivering live, interactive Kaplan–Meier visualizations tailored to individual patient profiles. His work turns complex population data into immediate, actionable insight.

Interns

Jonathan Gasaatura
Intern – Applied Analytics & Web Publishing

Jonathan joined Ukubona in Spring 2025 as the company’s first intern while a high school senior in Fairfax, VA. He delivered a full-stack analysis titled The Soto–Judge Effect, covering shifts in MLB hitting performance through lineup structure. His work combined statistical thinking with deployable tools — building for impact.


Paul Luswata
Intern – Data Analytics & Global Health Policy

Paul joined Ukubona in May 2025 to deepen his applied experience in data analytics and behavioral health policy. With a master’s background in health systems and Google Data Analytics certification, he contributes to dashboards, peer-reviewed writing, and research alongside doctoral studies in health policy.

Clinical Collaborators

Fawaz S. Al Ammary, MD, PhD
Living Donor Transplantation

Board-certified nephrologist and Associate Professor at UC Irvine School of Medicine. Former Medical Director of Living Donor Kidney Transplantation at Johns Hopkins. Collaborates with Ukubona on donor risk stratification, consent modeling, and ethical frameworks in transplant predictive analytics.


At Ukubona, we believe seeing is never passive. It is a continuous, recursive dance — attention focused, meaning interpreted, and action taken. This active cycle shapes not only how we perceive the world but how we engage with it, building decision systems that honor human complexity and nurture clarity in chaos.

“To see transcends looking — it is an active dance of attention, interpretation, and response.”
