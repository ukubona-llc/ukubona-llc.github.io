Meet Us

Company Ethos

Ukubona means “to see.” For us, seeing is recursive: play → learn → adjust → try again. We build decision systems where absolute risk is personalized from literature-grade multivariable models, and counterfactuals are simulated when data are sparse — so practice comes before prescription.


Founder

Abimereki Muzaale, MD, MPH — Founder & CEO

Clinical investigator and builder. Former NIH K-awardee, now focused on turning validated equations into individualized, explainable risk with counterfactual comparators — the backbone of our Game of Care engine and clinical tools.

Edward Ssemambo
Intern — Energy Systems & Simulation

Edward works at the substrate of intelligence: energy. Every new layer of simulation — from training AI to rehearsing clinical pathways — demands more power. Clarity is never free; it scales with supply and demand. His focus is on how photons, electrons, and bonds cascade into the cost of computation, making visible the economic and energetic price of vision. Environmental impact may be debated elsewhere — at Ukubona, the gradient is simple: intelligence consumes, and its cost must be counted.

A Note on Origins

Our name is a promise: to see clearly, then act — iteratively. The avatars in our Game of Care mirror how we work: play, learn, adjust, try again.
