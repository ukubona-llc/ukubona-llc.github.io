Meet Us

Our Ethos

At Ukubona, to see is to engage in a dynamic process — sensing patterns, weaving memory, applying meaning, and taking action.

Like Zarathustra’s ascent to greater heights, our team seeks clarity by seeing both long-term facts and counterfactual possibilities — gaining perspective that informs wiser, more adaptive decisions.

Spanning continents and disciplines, we unite in building decision systems that honor the recursive, embodied nature of human perception.


Founder Spotlight

Abimereki Muzaale brings a unique fusion of rigorous scientific inquiry, systems that learn and adapt over time, and a deep clarity shaped by experience.

From ages 26 to 45, his professional adulthood was forged at Johns Hopkins University — a tenure that solidified his expertise as a clinical investigator and public health leader. This foundation drives the vision and innovation at Ukubona LLC.

Focused on building interactive, personalized digital tools, Dr. Muzaale leads with a balance of disciplined inquiry and collaborative insight.


Leadership

Abimereki Muzaale, MD, MPH
Founder and CEO

Dr. Muzaale has been an NIH-funded clinical investigator for over 15 years with grants from NIDDK and NIA. He oversees initiatives focusing on decision-support and personalized risk-assessment, blending clinical insight with data-driven innovation.
