Our Models

Ukubona’s platform transforms complex statistical models into interactive, personalized decision-support tools. Below are examples of our current models, designed for high-stakes domains like living kidney donation and perioperative risk assessment.

Living Kidney Donor Risk Calculator

Built on Cox regression and survival analysis, this tool provides personalized risk projections for living kidney donors. It integrates curated datasets to visualize long-term health outcomes, emphasizing transparency in uncertainty.

Perioperative Risk Visualizer

This model translates surgical risk data into interactive visualizations, enabling clinicians and patients to co-navigate decision-making. It uses real-time data inputs to highlight key risk factors and outcomes.

Aging and Metabolic Self-Regulation

Our aging model-combines physiological and statistical data to support personalized health strategies. It provides responsive outputs for individuals and researchers studying metabolic self-regulation.
