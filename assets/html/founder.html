Founder & Visionary

Born in Alameda County, California to a doctoral scholar at UC Berkeley, our founder has spent the prime of their adult life—ages 26 to 45—shaping clinical research and public health at Johns Hopkins University. This tenure forged a foundation of rigorous inquiry and precision that fuels the innovation behind Ukubona LLC.

The vision driving Ukubona reflects a fusion of scientific discipline, recursive systems thinking, and ancestral clarity. While deeply rooted in the discipline of medicine and public health, it embraces a global perspective that values collaboration, diversity, and emergent insight.

At 45, our founder stands at the crossroads of experience and innovation, committed to building digital infrastructures that empower personalized medicine and decision-making — with clarity, trust, and recursion at the core.
