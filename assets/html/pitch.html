The Problem

High-stakes decisions (clinical, policy, operations) still rely on static PDFs and gut checks. Tools hide uncertainty, and when we do simulate, the real costs are invisible: energy, compute, and time.

Our Answer

Ukubona builds rehearsal spaces for decisions: validated models served via Flask APIs and rendered as interactive loops (avatars, comparators, sliders). We’re explicit that clarity costs energy and money. We expose those trade-offs so leaders can invest wisely.
