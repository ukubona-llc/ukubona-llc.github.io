The Game of Care

Healthcare reimagined as sequential, consequential play.

Choose Your Role

Why Gamify?

Regulation defines “advice.” Games define “play.” By gamifying the care pathway, Ukubona creates a safe, engaging ecosystem where patients, clinicians, insurers, and trainees all explore decisions — without claiming to treat, diagnose, or cure.

Not FDA Approved Not Medical Advice Interactive Learning & Simulation

Consequences & Levels

Every choice updates the underlying parameters and variance–covariance matrix. Like any good game, sequences matter: skip an immunization, delay a diagnosis, miss a payment — and you may find yourself “dead at Level 2.” Or you may discover resilience, redundancy, and recovery.

Advisors & Mentors

Guided by fictional luminaries like Zen Kiersen, MD and Jone Bopkin University, we poke fun at authority while building systems that, paradoxically, restore it. Investors should expect nothing less than the next Fortune 500 company emerging from the gaming community itself.

Ukubona LLC’s platform is a game. It is not FDA approved, and provides no medical advice. Participation is voluntary play. Outcomes are simulations, not clinical recommendations.
