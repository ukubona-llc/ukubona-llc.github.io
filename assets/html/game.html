Why Gamify?

Regulation defines "advice." Games define "play." By gamifying the care pathway, Ukubona creates a safe, engaging ecosystem where patients, clinicians, insurers, and trainees all explore decisions — without claiming to treat, diagnose, or cure.

To Screen or Not to Screen?

Doctor: Order a contrast CT now (rule-out value high) knowing a small allergy risk exists — or observe and re-evaluate?
Patient: Consent to the scan today — or delay for alternative tests and risk a missed diagnosis?
Insurer: Approve pre-auth — or request step-therapy and push the decision downstream?

Consequences & Levels

Every choice changes what comes next. Like any good game, sequences matter: skip an immunization, delay a diagnosis, miss a payment — and you may find yourself "dead at Level 2." Or you may uncover resilience, redundancy, and recovery.

Advisors & Mentors

Guided by fictional mentors, we poke fun at authority while building systems that, paradoxically, restore it. The aim is serious: a Fortune-500-class platform born from the gaming community itself.
