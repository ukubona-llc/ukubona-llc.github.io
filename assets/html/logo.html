The Logo is a Scout

Dynamic, not static — a sweep, not a stamp.

I  ·  The Emblem

The Radar at the Saddle Point

To See, Blindly

$$ T = 60\,\text{s} \quad|\quad \theta \in [0,\,2\pi] $$

Our logo is a scout in the stochastic wild. It sweeps 360° exactly once every 60 seconds — representing the Epoch. Each revolution is a full pass over the known, searching for signal inside the noise.

In high-dimensional optimization we operate under blindness. We cannot see the full map, so we actively interrogate the unknown using local gradients. The sweep is the search. The blip is the signal.

II  ·  The Method

Stochastic Foraging

The Scout's Dilemma

$$ \mathbb{E}[\nabla Q(\theta)] \approx \frac{1}{m} \sum_{i=1}^{m} \nabla\, l\!\left(f_{\theta}(x_i),\, y_i\right) $$

We accept blindness. Instead of demanding the full landscape, we take small mini-batches of reality and estimate the local gradient beneath our feet. This is the core of Stochastic Gradient Descent.

The logo encodes this epistemology: visibility is earned one sweep at a time, never granted wholesale.

III  ·  The Cadence

The Compass of 60 Seconds

Time as the Forcing Function

$$ \omega = \frac{2\pi}{60} \approx 0.1047\ \text{rad/s} $$

The 60-second cycle enforces a rhythm. Spin too fast → noise overwhelms signal. Spin too slow → drift obscures gradient.

Ukubona is the disciplined calibration between exploration and exploitation — neither rushing past the signal nor waiting for certainty that never comes.
