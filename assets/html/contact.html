Frequently Asked Questions

Can you help with my specific clinical case?

We don’t provide clinical advice. We build rehearsal tools and model interfaces that clinicians and patients can co-navigate.

Do you work internationally?

Yes. We operate remotely and collaborate across time zones with partners worldwide.

What about data privacy and security?

We follow least-privilege access, disclosure controls (k-anon, noise, redaction), and auditable policies. We can work within your institution’s requirements.

Are there internship opportunities?

Occasionally — project-based mentorship focused on models, APIs, and decision UI. Tell us your angle.
