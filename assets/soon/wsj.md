The Bantu root **-bon-** runs deep:

* `Ukubona` (Zulu): to see, to perceive
* `Okubona` (Soga): what you see
* `Kuona` (Swahili): to see (passively)
* `Kutazama` (Swahili): to watch (actively)
* `Okubonabona` (Soga, Ganda): recursive seeing (to see each other repeatedly or intensely)
* `Kubona` (Runyakole): to find, to notice

**🧠 Pattern:** This is **perception as process**, not just input. “To see” is not passive—it is recursive, affective, and active when done with intention.
