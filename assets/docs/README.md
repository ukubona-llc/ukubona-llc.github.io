Good question — **“personalized”** and **“customized”** are often used interchangeably in health tech and marketing copy, but they signal slightly different things. Here’s the distinction you might want to keep in mind when deciding what *Ukubona LLC* should emphasize in your hero text:

---

### 🔹 Personalized

* **Definition**: Adapted to an *individual’s intrinsic data*, traits, or context.
* **In health tech**: Care plans shaped by a patient’s genomics, vitals, behavior, environment, or risk profiles.
* **Implication**: Feels *organic* and data-driven — the system “knows me.”
* **Tagline fit**: “Personalized care” resonates with *precision medicine* and your “digital twin” ethos.

---

### 🔹 Customized

* **Definition**: Modified or chosen *by the user* from available options.
* **In health tech**: Selecting preferred reminders, interface themes, or plan modules.
* **Implication**: Feels *user-driven* and flexible — *I choose what fits me.*
* **Tagline fit**: “Customized care” would stress patient empowerment and configurability.

---

### 🔹 Quick Example

* **Personalized**: AI adjusts your walking-speed baseline because your frailty phenotype shows early decline.
* **Customized**: You set your own alert frequency for step-count goals.
